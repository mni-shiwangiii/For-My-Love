Someone Like You ❣️

Our friends have theories, they think they know, But we're just two weirdos stealing the show. You call me names, I give them back, Our friendship's a train that's never on track. You make me smile without even trying, And make me laugh when I'm busy denying. We're more than just jokes, but less than a label, Two stubborn fools with a bond that's unstable. Between Buses and Cars

I just want to tell you that, for me, you're the best.
